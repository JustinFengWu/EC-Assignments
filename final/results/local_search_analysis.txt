Summary:
Across all datasets, the 2-opt method consistently outperformed exchange and jump, achieving the lowest minimum and mean tour lengths.
Exchange performed the worst, often producing significantly longer tours, especially in larger instances (e.g., pr2392, usa13509).
Jump's results were generally between 2-opt and exchange but did not surpass 2-opt in any case.
The performance gap was more pronounced for larger problem sizes, highlighting 2-opt’s efficiency in improving tours.
