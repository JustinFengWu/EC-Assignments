The Inver-over algorithm performed better than our evolutionary algorithm on smaller instances such as eil51, eil76, and eil101, showing lower average tour costs and smaller standard deviations.
For medium-sized problems like kroC100 and kroD100, the results were mixed, with our EA sometimes giving slightly better solutions but with more variability.
On larger instances such as pr2392 and usa13509, our evolutionary algorithm clearly outperformed Inver-over, obtaining much lower average costs.
Overall, Inver-over seems well-suited for small to medium TSP instances, while our evolutionary approach is more robust and effective on larger problems.
